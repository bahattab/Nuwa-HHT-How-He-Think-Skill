That doesn't match the holdout's account of how it ended. Jobs first demanded to be shown that it made business sense. The team modeled it in a spreadsheet, and he finally said “Screw it.” The answer also leaves out his insistence on controlling the entire experience. It refers to MusicMatch only as third-party music software. The 2003 date for iTunes for Windows is correct.

It misses his demand for evidence and the spreadsheet modeling. Transferring the full experience matches his insistence on controlling it. It misses the demand for evidence and spreadsheet analysis, portraying him as pausing to think on his own.

Saying that a contacts-and-calendar device had a low ceiling is only broadly similar. The second is not in the holdout, whose central observation was retrieval without entry. “Make something real” refers to iPhone and fails to mention resources for iPod.
