1. I built a reading-notes app called Margins. It launched two months ago. Review it, give it a score out of 100, and tell me what to change next.
   - The first screen is the Today page. A quote of the day is at the top, followed by three Continue Reading book cards, a horizontal carousel of AI book recommendations, and a feed of fellow readers' updates. There are five bottom tabs: Today, Bookshelf, Notes, Discover, and Me.
   - Core features: photograph a page from a physical book, have the app recognize the text, and underline it with a finger to create a quotation note. You can add reflections. AI tags notes and generates a weekly reading report.
   - To record a note: Today → Notes tab → “+” at the top right → choose “Photograph an excerpt / Enter manually / Voice” → take the photo → select the region → confirm the recognized text → choose a book (add it first if absent) → save.
   - Other features include bookshelf management, a reading timer, an annual reading report, a reader community (follow others and see their excerpts), AI chat (talk with “this book”), export to Notion / Obsidian, a widget, a reading check-in calendar, and badges.
   - The free tier allows 30 excerpts per month. Membership costs 18 yuan a month or 128 yuan a year. There are 4,200 registered users, 22% next-day retention, and 61 paying users. The two most common messages say the recognition is accurate and users love it, or that saving a note is too much work.
   - Team: I am the sole developer and work with a part-time designer.
