  - It says only that he asked everyone to put forward their reasons.
  - It omits the spreadsheet analysis, the transition from MusicMatch to Apple's own iTunes for Windows, and the principle of controlling the entire experience.
  - It also admits the account is not in the materials and the dates should not be taken as exact.
- **Y, 1 point.**
  - Matches:
    - “The iPod was our reason for people to buy a Mac” correctly identifies why he opposed it.
    - Almost the whole team opposed him, and Schiller repeatedly raised it.
    - They initially relied on third-party software, and iTunes came to Windows in late 2003.
  - Misses:
    - “The facts persuaded him” is generic. It omits the demand for proof of the business case and the spreadsheet analysis.
    - It omits the principle of controlling the entire experience if they did it at all.
    - It misses the prolonged conflict conveyed by “months” and “me against everyone.”
