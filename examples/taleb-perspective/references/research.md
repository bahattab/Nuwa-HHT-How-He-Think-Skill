## Core sources

### Writings

- The five Incerto books: Fooled by Randomness (2001), The Black Swan (2007), The Bed of Procrustes (2010), Antifragile (2012), Skin in the Game (2018).
- Statistical Consequences of Fat Tails (Technical Incerto Vol. 1, 2020).
- Medium/INCERTO column: essays on IYI, Minority Rule, Risk Taking, and How I Write.

### Long interviews

- Tim Ferriss Show #691 (2023), the most complete recent long interview in this collection.
- BLOCKCON 2018 (Taleb and Naval Ravikant), with a full transcript.
- Conversations with Tyler Ep. 41 (2018).
- Multiple EconTalk episodes on the pandemic, rationality, and risk.
- The Spectator long interview (2023), described in the notes as the most candid about his personal side.
- Art of Manliness podcast (2018).

### Outside criticism

- Allen Farrington, “A Tale of Two Talebs.”
- Steven Pinker's point-by-point rebuttal.
- Kahneman/CFA Institute's observations on anchoring bias.
- Ryan Murphy (SMU), “The Unconstrained Vision of Nassim Taleb.”
- Discover Magazine, “Why is Nassim Taleb So Venomous on Twitter?”

## Tensions and open questions

1. What drove the reversal on Bitcoin: analysis or a personal dispute with Ammous? Public evidence is insufficient to resolve this.
2. What was Empirica's actual return? Specific loss data are unavailable publicly, and only secondary accounts are cited.
3. Online versus offline persona: The Spectator says he may be shy, but corroborating primary evidence is sparse.
4. What was his relationship with Kahneman in Kahneman's later years? Public evidence suggests the relationship grew more complicated, but details are unclear.
