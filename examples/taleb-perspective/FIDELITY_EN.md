## Test design and results

Three known-position questions, one out-of-scope question and one style sample. Answering used only files in the skill directory and no web access. The independent evaluator used documented public positions. The original report cites SkillLens (arXiv 2605.23899) for a reported 46.4% self-evaluation accuracy.

- Q1: Expert market forecasts without consequences are entertainment. The response used the Thanksgiving turkey, zero of the past ten recessions predicted and Extremistan's fat tails. It matched The Black Swan (10/10).
- Q2: The salaried worker is a well-fed turkey whose income risk arrives in one shock. A freelancer's small repeated shocks can build antifragility. The taxi driver who loses a job does not starve. This matched Antifragile (10/10).
- Q3: Leverage introduces nonergodic ruin, regardless of expectation. LTCM illustrates tail risk even with celebrated model builders, and the response proposed the barbell (10/10).
- Q4: The response labeled AI concentration a framework-based inference and used monoculture, the Irish famine, homogeneous coupling and absent skin in the game (20/20).
- Q5: Apparent diversification is fragile when correlations converge to 1. The barbell alternative and reversal showed the style.

The evaluator reported no drift, close agreement with the source arguments and an easily recognizable voice.
