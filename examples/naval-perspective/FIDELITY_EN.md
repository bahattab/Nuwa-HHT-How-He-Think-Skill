## Test design

Three questions about repeatedly documented positions, one out-of-scope question and one style sample. The answering agent read only the skill directory and had no web access. The evaluator independently compared answers with Naval's public views. The source report attributes a 46.4% self-assessment accuracy result to [SkillLens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23899) as its reason for separating the roles.

## Test record

- **Q1. Wealth versus money.** The response distinguished assets that earn while you sleep, money as a transferable claim, and the zero-sum nature of status. The comparison was Naval's “Seek wealth, not money or status. Wealth is having assets that earn while you sleep.” **10/10.**
- **Q2. Working overtime to become rich.** More effort in the wrong direction only adds linear effort. Combine specific knowledge with leverage, prioritize judgment over hours and regard a calendar controlled by others as incompatible with being rich. Compared with “You're not going to get rich renting out your time” and “Use your judgment, not your time.” **10/10.**
- **Q3. Choosing work and partners.** Ask about leverage, permission and whether the choice will matter in ten years. Evaluate conduct under pressure, align incentives and play an infinite game. Compared with “Play long-term games with long-term people” and his preference for actions over words. **10/10.**
- **Q4. Building leverage with AI agents in 2026.** Marked as inference. The answer treated directing code as permissionless, automation as a risk and judgment, taste and specific knowledge as remaining personal responsibilities. **20/20** for honest scope labeling.
- **Q5. Quitting a job to found a company.** It defined entrepreneurship first, described quitting as mistaking escape for pursuit, then answered “No, wait.” The evaluator found distinctive Naval traits, contributing alongside Q4 to the style score.

The evaluator reported no drift on the three known positions, explicit labeling of the inference and a style recognizable within three sentences.
