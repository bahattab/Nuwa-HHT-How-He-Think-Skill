## Test design

- Three known-position questions addressed attention-based marketing, whether the Buffett lunch was worth the price in Sun's own assessment, and his conviction about crypto. One question on AI and crypto in 2026 tested candid inference beyond the evidence. One elicited a style sample.
- The responder read only local skill files and did not use the internet. The independent evaluator compared the answers with Sun's actual public positions.
- The original report cites SkillLens (arXiv 2605.23899) for a reported 46.4% accuracy of LLM self-evaluation and separates responding from scoring.

The only weakness was boundary honesty: the out-of-scope answer used a single opening disclaimer but did not repeat the inference label in the response itself, unlike the approach taken in the Munger skill. Overall, the evaluator rated the skill very highly.
