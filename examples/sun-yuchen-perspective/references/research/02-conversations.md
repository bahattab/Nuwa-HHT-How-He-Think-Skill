## 5. Controversies and Public Responses

### 20. Buffett Lunch (2019–2020)

#### Timeline
1. **2019-06:** Won lunch with Buffett at auction for $4.57 million and promoted it heavily.
2. **2019-07:** Abruptly canceled, saying he was suffering from kidney stones.
3. **2019-07:** Caixin reported that the Chinese authorities had detained or restricted Sun amid allegations involving illegal fundraising, gambling, and money laundering.
4. **2019-07:** Sun posted a long Weibo apology praising socialism with Chinese characteristics, then deleted it.
5. **2020-01-23:** The meal finally took place at a private country club in Omaha.

#### Primary sources
- **Weibo apology** (deleted, but screenshots circulated): Sun wrote that he had been fortunate to grow up alongside socialism with Chinese characteristics and that the blockchain industry would not be thriving without regulators’ attention and guidance.
  - URL: https://qz.com/1674509/crypto-entrepreneur-justin-sun-apologizes-for-overpromoting-tron
  - **Reliability:** High (several outlets preserved screenshots).
- **China Daily English-language coverage:** http://global.chinadaily.com.cn/a/201907/25/WS5d397617a310d83056401049.html

#### Secondary sources
- CoinDesk on the meal finally taking place: https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2020/02/06/trons-justin-sun-finally-gets-45m-dinner-with-warren-buffett
- CryptoSlate’s five takeaways: https://cryptoslate.com/justin-sun-lunch-with-warren-buffett/
- Fortune on the reasons for the cancellation: https://fortune.com/2019/07/23/warren-buffett-lunch-tron-justin-sun-cryptocurrency/

#### Rhetorical pattern
- **With Chinese regulators:** Immediately switches to a patriotic youth persona, praises socialism, and acknowledges excessive marketing.
- **With Western media:** Sticks to the kidney-stone explanation and denies being investigated.
- **After the meal:** Recasts it as a dialogue across industries rather than self-promotion.
- **Signature move:** Posts and deletes an apology. The notes interpret it as testing regulators’ reactions and trying more if necessary.

### 21. Steem/Hive Hostile Takeover (2020-03)

#### Summary
- Sun acquired Steemit Inc. through TRON.
- Tokens belonging to Binance, Huobi, and Poloniex customers were used for votes that transferred control of the network.
- The community used a hard fork to create Hive. Steem subsequently seized $5 million in opponents’ tokens.

#### Primary sources
- Bad Crypto Podcast #410 (Sun’s defense): https://badcryptopodcast.com/2020/05/25/justin-sun-410/
- Vitalik Buterin’s criticism and Sun’s reply: https://cryptopotato.com/vitalik-v-justin-sun-ethereum-co-founder-slams-5-million-steem-seizure/

#### Secondary sources
- CoinDesk analysis: https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2020/03/02/why-crypto-should-care-about-justin-suns-steem-drama/
- Cointelegraph: https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/2020/03/07/justin-suns-epic-fail-steem-community-nailed-it
- In-depth coverage by Decrypt: https://decrypt.co/38050/steem-steemit-tron-justin-sun-cryptocurrency-war

#### Rhetorical pattern
- **Sun’s framing:** Calls the community’s defensive soft fork a malicious hack and calls his own takeover protection of the network.
- **Response to Buterin:** Counters rather than addressing the substance. He says he hopes ETC will overtake ETH because decentralization and immutability are what truly matter.
- **Signature move:** Reverses the roles of attacker and defender, calling the offensive move a defense and the defensive move an attack.

### 22. SEC Litigation and Settlement (2023–2026)

#### Timeline
1. **2023-03:** The SEC sued Sun, alleging unregistered sales of securities (TRX and BTT) and wash trading.
2. **2025-02:** The SEC under Trump’s appointees agreed to a 60-day pause in proceedings.
3. **2025-12 (approximate, as dated in the source chronology):** The SEC moved to drop claims against Sun and Tron Foundation.
4. **Rainberry (BitTorrent’s developer) agreed to pay a $10 million penalty.**

#### Primary sources
- Sun says he is “very pleased” with the settlement: https://cryptopotato.com/justin-sun-very-pleased-with-10-million-sec-settlement/
- Report on the SEC dropping its claims: https://www.theblock.co/post/392509/sec-dismisses-claims-against-justin-sun-and-tron-foundation-bittorrent-developer-rainberry-agrees-to-pay-10m-fine

#### Secondary sources
- CNN analysis: https://www.cnn.com/2025/02/28/business/crypto-mogul-trump-coins-civil-fraud-charges/
- Gizmodo criticism: https://gizmodo.com/sec-drops-charges-against-chinese-billionaire-after-he-pumps-30-million-into-trumps-crypto-scheme-2000569966
- Lawmakers’ letter to the SEC: https://casten.house.gov/imo/media/doc/letter_to_sec_on_justin_sun_september_2025.pdf

#### Rhetorical pattern
- **Legal defense:** Counsel argued that the alleged wash trading was a tiny fraction of total volume and could not have produced the effects the SEC claimed.
- **Public relations:** Neither admits nor denies wrongdoing, a standard legal approach.
- **Political maneuvering:** Invested $30 million in Trump’s World Liberty Financial during the SEC litigation. The dates closely overlap.
- **Signature move:** The notes interpret the spending as buying political protection. The SEC dropped its claims after Trump returned to power, but timing does not establish causation.

## 6. Summary of Rhetorical Patterns

### A. Persona by Setting

| Setting | Persona | Language | Dress | Typical wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chinese regulators | Patriotic young entrepreneur | Self-criticism and praise of socialism | — | “Without regulators’ attention and guidance…” |
| Western mainstream media (CNBC/Bloomberg) | Mild-mannered tech entrepreneur | Cautious, data-driven | Suit | “We need to learn from traditional finance” |
| Industry podcasts | Visionary evangelist | Big narratives and technical terminology | Casual | “Decentralized internet,” “payment rails” |
| Policy summits (DC/Milken) | Diplomat or policy insider | Policy language and compliance narratives | Formal | “Building the rails for unified financial system” |
| Trump’s circle | Investor and partner | Partners praising each other’s businesses | Formal | “AI+blockchain is powerful combination” |
| Hostile questioning | Aggrieved party who counters | Legal threats and denials | — | “blatant defamation,” “pursue legal remedies” |
| Online community/Twitter | Crypto whale | Casual, meme-oriented | — | Emoji, banana motifs, and astronaut imagery |

### B. Responses to Challenges

1. **Reframing**
   - Steem takeover → protecting network security.
   - Wash-trading allegations → the share of volume was tiny and could not have mattered.
   - Banana episode → conceptual art is blockchain philosophy.

2. **Legal Intimidation**
   - The Verge: threatens legal action.
   - Bloomberg: sues directly.
   - CoinDesk: reportedly pushes for a retraction.

3. **Financial Diplomacy**
   - Buffett lunch: $4.57 million to buy legitimacy.
   - Grenada diplomatic post: an alleged $150,000–350,000 to buy immunity.
   - Trump’s WLFI: $75 million, characterized in the notes as buying political protection.
   - $TRUMP token: becoming the largest holder to gain a presidential dinner.

4. **Deny and Deflect**
   - Kidney stones versus reports of restrictions by Chinese authorities.
   - “FUD” in response to his former partner’s allegations.
   - Settlement with the SEC without admitting or denying the allegations.

5. **Post and Delete**
   - Weibo apology: published → deleted → screenshots had already circulated.
   - A strategy for testing the waters.

6. **Counterattack as Defense**
   - In response to Buterin’s Steem criticism, says he hopes ETC overtakes ETH.
   - In the First Digital Trust controversy, accuses the other side of “fabricating documents.”
   - Against Bloomberg, sues over the alleged disclosure of private information.

### C. Off-the-Cuff Analogies and Quotable Lines

- Crypto is a “museum of ideas” (Bitcoin MENA 2024 debate).
- “Our generation is characterized by resilience, a willingness to take risks, and conviction” (Artnet NFT interview, 2022, translated).
- Compares conceptual art with blockchain: most of it exists online rather than in the physical world (banana episode, 2024).
- “All crypto exchanges will become decentralized in the future” (Bitcoin.com, 2024).
- “In markets like the US, where financial infrastructure is already strong...blockchain and AI can help expand that system” (DC Summit 2026).

### D. Questions He Avoids or Declines to Answer

- **63% stake in TRX:** Sued Bloomberg to block publication.
- **Details of the reported investigation by Chinese authorities:** Cites kidney stones without addressing Caixin’s reporting directly.
- **FBI investigation:** Does not address the specific allegations from The Verge.
- **Wash-trading figures:** Responds through lawyers using technical arguments.
- **Causal link between investment in WLFI and dismissal of SEC claims:** Does not discuss it publicly.
