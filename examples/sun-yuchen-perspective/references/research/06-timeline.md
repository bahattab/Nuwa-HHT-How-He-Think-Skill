# Justin Sun — Complete Timeline

## 1. Upbringing and education (1990–2014)

**July 30, 1990** — Born in Xining, Qinghai Province, China. At age four, he moved with his family to Huizhou, Guangdong. According to media reports, family upheaval in his teens—his mother remarried and moved abroad, and his relationship with his father was strained—coincided with a period of heavy gaming, skipped classes, and grades falling below the cutoff for third-tier undergraduate admissions.

**2007** — After studying the patterns of past winning essays, he entered the New Concept Writing Competition and took first prize. This was a crucial stepping stone to Peking University.

**2007–2008** — He used his Qinghai household registration to sit the national university entrance exam, a practice observers dubbed “gaokao migration.” He scored 650, three points below Peking University’s cutoff, but gained admission to the **Department of History at Peking University** through his New Concept first prize and bonus points from the university’s independent admissions process. (Sources: China News Service, Sohu Education, Sina Education)

**2008–2011** — He studied history at Peking University. By his own account, he graduated with the department’s highest GPA by regularly engaging with professors and submitting papers for review ahead of time, among other practices. He appeared on the cover of Asia Weekly in 2011.

**2011–2013** — He pursued a **Master of Arts in East Asian Studies at the University of Pennsylvania**. There he encountered Bitcoin and began investing in cryptocurrency.

**2013** — He joined **Ripple Labs** as its chief representative and adviser for Greater China, marking his entry into the blockchain industry.

**2014** — The World Economic Forum (Davos) named him a Global Shaper. That year he returned to China to found **Ruibo Technology**, a Ripple-affiliated Chinese project focused on cross-border bank clearing and settlement.

## 2. Early entrepreneurship and the “Jack Ma protégé” label (2015–2016)

**Early 2015** — Ruibo Technology raised a **Series A in the tens of millions of US dollars**, led by ChinaEquity Capital with participation from IDG Capital and others.

**2015** — He joined the inaugural cohort at **Hupan University**, founded by Jack Ma, as its only participant born in the 1990s. That connection later became a recurring marketing story and gave rise to the “Jack Ma protégé” label. The same year, he was named to **Forbes China’s 30 Under 30 list of entrepreneurs**.

**2015** — He launched **the Peiwo app**, designed for instant voice chats between strangers and distinguished by zodiac-based matching and paid calls. The product did not go on to achieve large-scale success.

## 3. TRON and its ICO (2017)

**July 2017** — He established the **TRON Foundation**, positioning it as a decentralized platform for distributing content.

**September 2017** — TRON held an **initial coin offering (ICO)** for its TRX token and raised about **$70 million**. According to The Verge, Sun completed the token sale days before the Chinese government announced its ICO ban and was already aware of the impending ban. (Sources: The Verge, Wikipedia)

**Late 2017** — TRX surged in price and briefly entered the top ten by market capitalization. The white paper was accused of extensively copying Ethereum, IPFS, and other projects. Sun later attributed the similarities to a “translation issue.”

## 4. BitTorrent acquisition and mainnet launch (2018)

**January 2018** — A letter of intent to acquire BitTorrent was signed. Negotiations reportedly began as early as September 2017.

**May 2018** — TRON launched its mainnet, migrating from Ethereum to an independent blockchain.

**June 2018** — The **$140 million** acquisition of **BitTorrent, Inc.** (later renamed Rainberry Inc.) was completed. BitTorrent founder Bram Cohen subsequently left and started the cryptocurrency project Chia. (Sources: Variety, Finance Magnates)

**July 2018** — The TRON Foundation officially announced that the BitTorrent acquisition had closed.

## 5. The Buffett lunch episode (2019)

**June 2019** — Sun won a charity lunch with Warren Buffett with a record **$4.567 million** bid. He publicized it with a prominent Twitter countdown and invited multiple figures in the crypto industry, including Litecoin founder Charlie Lee. (Sources: Fortune, CNBC)

**July 22, 2019** — He abruptly canceled the lunch, citing “kidney stones.”

**July 23–25, 2019** — Events took a sharp turn. Citing Chinese media, CoinDesk reported that Sun had been “barred from leaving the country.” Sun then released an apology acknowledging excessive hype and immature behavior. (Sources: Washington Post, Bloomberg, CNN)

**January 23, 2020** — The lunch finally took place as a dinner in Omaha, Nebraska. Guests included Charlie Lee and eToro CEO Yoni Assia. Buffett later said that “cryptocurrencies have no value” but did not criticize Sun personally. (Sources: CoinDesk, CNBC)

## 6. Controversy over the Steem/Steemit takeover (2020)

**February 2020** — The TRON Foundation acquired Steemit from its founder, Ned Scott, for about **$8 million**, obtaining around **70 million STEEM tokens** (about 20% of total supply).

**February 24, 2020** — Steem community validators implemented a soft fork to freeze the voting power of Steemit’s tokens.

**Early March 2020** — Sun teamed up with three major exchanges, **Binance, Huobi, and Poloniex**, to vote with STEEM tokens deposited by users. They replaced all 20 top witness nodes at once in what was described as a “hostile takeover.” (Sources: CoinDesk, Decrypt, CoinTelegraph)

**March 20, 2020** — The Steem community hard-forked to create the **Hive** blockchain, and most members of the original community migrated there.

## 7. Exchange empire and DeFi/NFT (2019–2022)

**2019** — Sun acquired a controlling stake in the cryptocurrency exchange **Poloniex**.

**2021** — Grenada appointed him **Ambassador and Permanent Representative to the WTO**, with the title “His Excellency.”

**2021** — He launched DeFi projects in the TRON ecosystem, including JustLend and SUN.io, and launched the algorithmic stablecoin **USDD**.

**October 2021** — He participated in Animoca Brands’ **$65 million funding round**.

**2022** — Through About Capital Management, he acquired the **Huobi** exchange (later renamed HTX) at a valuation of about **$3 billion**. Initially described as a “global adviser,” the timeline characterizes him as the person exercising effective control. (Sources: Bloomberg, TechCrunch)

## 8. SEC lawsuit and loss of diplomatic status (2023)

**March 2023** — The US SEC sued Sun in federal court in the Southern District of New York, alleging:
- TRX and BTT were unregistered securities.
- Wash trading was used to manipulate the TRX market and inflate trading volume by **$31 million**.
- Celebrities were paid to promote the tokens without disclosing that they had been compensated.
(Sources: Official SEC filings, Fortune, CoinDesk)

**March 31, 2023** — Grenada’s government ended Sun’s appointment as WTO ambassador. (Sources: Fortune Crypto, Protos)

**November 2023** — The Poloniex exchange was hacked and lost more than **$125 million**. (Sources: Decrypt, SiliconANGLE)

## 9. Art collecting and ties to Trump (2024–2025)

**November 2024** — Sun bought “Comedian,” the banana artwork, at Sotheby’s for **$6.2 million** and later ate the banana in public. (Sources: Sportskeeda, Slate, Cryptonomist)

**November 2024–January 2025** — He invested about **$75 million** in total in the Trump family’s **World Liberty Financial (WLFI)**, becoming its largest single investor. (Sources: Fortune Crypto, CoinDesk)

**April–May 2025** — He purchased substantial quantities of the **$TRUMP** memecoin, becoming the highest-ranked holder with a position worth approximately **$18.6 million**.

**May 22, 2025** — He attended Trump’s dinner for top $TRUMP holders. He reportedly received a gold watch. (Sources: CNBC, CNN, Washington Post)

**September 2025** — WLFI froze $9 million in WLFI tokens that Sun had transferred to HTX. Members of Congress wrote to the SEC requesting an investigation. (Sources: Fortune, Axios)

## 10. SEC settlement and latest developments (2026)

**March 5, 2026** — The SEC reached a **$10 million settlement** with Sun, who did not admit to wrongdoing. The settlement was widely seen as a marked shift in the SEC’s approach to crypto enforcement under the Trump administration. (Sources: US News, CoinDesk, Politico Pro)

**As of April 2026** — TRON had surpassed **373 million** cumulative users and 13 billion transactions. Its on-chain USDT supply exceeded **$85 billion**, making it the largest circulating network for USDT globally. Sun’s estimated personal net worth ranged from **$5 billion to $12.5 billion**. (Sources: Arkham Intelligence, CoinCodex, MEXC)

## Analysis of key turning points

1. **2017 ICO** — Sun moved from conventional entrepreneurship into cryptocurrency and built his initial pool of capital.
2. **2019 Buffett lunch** — He went from crypto celebrity to a figure of global interest, but the episode also exposed what made him controversial.
3. **2020 Steem episode** — In the timeline’s reading, it exposed how a self-described “evangelist for decentralization” relied on centralized power.
4. **2023 SEC lawsuit** — Facing his most serious legal challenge, he shifted toward investing in political relationships.
5. **2024–2025 cultivation of ties to Trump** — The timeline treats this as a strategic pivot to navigate legal risk.
