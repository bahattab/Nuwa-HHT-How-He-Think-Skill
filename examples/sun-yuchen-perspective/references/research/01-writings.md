## 1. Formal Publications

### 1.1 *The World Is Both Cruel and Tender* (2017)

- **Genre:** Autobiography and motivational writing.
- **Publisher:** Jiuzhou Press, March 2017.
- **Pages and price:** 288 pages, list price 39.80 yuan.
- **ISBN:** 9787510850660.
- **Foreword:** Feng Lun, founder of Vantone Group.
- **Core narrative:** “In ten years: from the cutoff for third-tier colleges to Peking University, from studying in the US to founding a business back home, from having nothing to financial freedom.”
- **Chapter themes:** Choosing your own dream, declining a safe job, independence, investment and entrepreneurship, and financial freedom.
- **Key lines:**
  - “Competition in this era is fiercer, growth more untamed, rewards richer, and risks more staggering.”
  - “The most important thing in life is the scope of your perspective. Like a species’ genes, it determines growth from the very beginning.”
- **Narrative analysis:** The book established Sun’s persona as an outsider who beat the odds. It recounts a complete story of upward mobility, from a student at the third-tier admissions threshold to Peking University, Penn, and financial freedom.
- **Sources:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/%E8%BF%99%E4%B8%96%E7%95%8C%E6%97%A2%E6%AE%8B%E9%85%B7%E4%B9%9F%E6%B8%A9%E6%9F%94-%E5%AD%99%E5%AE%87%E6%99%A8/dp/B01LTICE1E) / [Douban](https://book.douban.com/subject/26984629/) / [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/147050303)
- **Reliability:** Primary source (his own book).

### 1.2 *Blockchain and the New Digital World* (2023)

- **Genre:** Industry monograph.
- **Publisher:** Contemporary World Press, January 2023.
- **Platforms:** Dangdang, iReader, WeRead, and others.
- **Structure** (five chapters):
  1. The arrival of the digital wave.
  2. The transformation of trust mechanisms.
  3. The evolution of information networks.
  4. Rebuilding financial networks.
  5. Rebuilding the digital world.

- **Sources:** [WeRead](https://weread.qq.com/web/reader/662327d0813ab7d69g01922b) / [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/newsletter/detail_24922.html) / [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/%E5%8C%BA%E5%9D%97%E9%93%BE%E4%B8%8E%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%97%E6%96%B0%E4%B8%96%E7%95%8C.html?id=u6Rb0AEACAAJ)

## 4. Technical White Papers

### 4.1 TRON White Paper (2017)

- **Published:** 2017.
- **Core proposal:** Build infrastructure for a decentralized internet.
- **Plagiarism controversy (January 2018):**
  - **Accuser:** Protocol Labs founder Juan Benet said on Twitter that at least nine pages of the English TRON white paper copied material from the IPFS or Filecoin papers.
  - **Independent check:** BuzzFeed News commissioned Quetext to check for plagiarism and reported a similarity score above 15%, including identical passages, diagrams, and phrases.
  - **Vitalik Buterin’s response:** The Ethereum co-founder joked that TRON improved the efficiency of writing the white paper with “Ctrl+C + Ctrl+V.”
  - **Sun’s response:** Said the Chinese paper had detailed citations and that volunteer translators omitted important details from the English version.
- **Core contradiction:** The project claimed technical innovation, but independent analysis identified substantial overlap in its foundational technical document and the notes regard the response as evasive.
- **Sources:** [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/ryanmac/cryptocurrency-white-paper-plagiarism-tron-justin-sun) / [CoinCentral](https://coincentral.com/community-accuses-tron-plagiarizing-whitepaper/) / [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/justin-suns-controversies-plagiarism-teslas-warren-buffett-kidney-stones-and-a-deleted-apology/) / [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/hardfork/2018/01/15/cryptocurrency-startup-tron-disaster/)
- **Reliability:** Primary document and independent analysis of the document.

## 5. Open Letters and Statements

### 5.1 USDD Open Letter (April 2022)

- **Published:** April 21, 2022.
- **Platforms:** Twitter and BusinessWire.
- **Key points:**
  - Announced the decentralized stablecoin USDD (Decentralized USD) on the TRON blockchain.
  - TRON DAO would work with industry leaders to establish USDD.
  - An algorithm would maintain a 1:1 peg to the dollar.
  - The plan called for $10 billion in crypto collateral.
  - The TRON DAO Reserve would be created to ensure sustainability.
- **Key wording:** “leveraging the power of mathematics and algorithms to achieve the overarching goal of financial freedom for all”
- **Later controversies:**
  - USDD removed $732 million in bitcoin collateral without fanfare in 2024.
  - The promised $10 billion reserve was never delivered, according to the notes.
- **Narrative analysis:** Framed the stablecoin as a movement for financial freedom for all. The announcement came one month before the Terra/LUNA collapse.
- **Sources:** [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220421005765/en) / [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/142867/justin-sun-to-launch-algorithmic-stablecoin-usdd-on-tron-will-use-10-billion-of-crypto-as-collateral) / [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/defi/justin-sun-responds-after-usdd-stablecoin-removes-bitcoin/) / [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2022/05/14/justin-sun-talks-usdd-stablecoin-in-wake-of-lunaust-unravel)

## 9. Recurring Core Arguments (Three or More Occurrences)

### 9.1 Decentralization Narrative

- **Frequency:** Appears in nearly all his public appearances.
- **Core formulation:** The decentralized internet is the future, and blockchain will replace traditional financial infrastructure.
- **Core contradiction:** While championing decentralization, he reportedly controls most TRON tokens and centralized exchanges including HTX and Poloniex. Critics called it a “quasi-feudal system,” whose economics and governance, though nominally decentralized, are dictated by one person.
- **Sources:** [Medium/Global Times Singapore](https://medium.com/@GlobalTimesSingapore/justin-sun-wants-to-control-the-future-of-crypto-the-question-is-how-much-he-already-does-5ca818c38536) / [Protos](https://protos.com/justin-sun-reveals-poloniex-issues-prevent-proof-of-reserves/)
- **Reliability:** Secondary sources (investigative reporting and legal filings).

### 9.2 Financial Freedom

- **Frequency:** Recurs in book titles, podcast titles, the USDD open letter, and speeches.
- **Core formulation:** Financial freedom is the ability not to sell out your values for money.
- **Evolution:** Personal motivation (2017 autobiography) → paid content (Ximalaya) → project narrative (USDD’s “financial freedom for all”).

### 9.3 Jack Ma’s Protégé Persona

### 9.4 All-In Gambler Philosophy

- **Frequency:** Appears repeatedly in the autobiography, interviews, and analysis.

### 9.5 Attention-Hijacking Marketing

- **Frequency:** Runs through his entire public career.
- **Signature events:**
  1. Paid $4.56 million for the Buffett lunch at auction (2019) → canceled citing kidney stones → became a global trending story → attended in January 2020, with dinner lasting over three hours.
  2. Won Cattelan’s *Comedian* for $6.24 million (2024) → paid in crypto → ate the banana publicly at Hong Kong’s most expensive hotel, according to the notes → likened conceptual art to crypto → reportedly sought to suppress a critical CoinDesk article.

3. **Stacking prestigious associations:** Peking University, Penn, Davos, Hupan, Jack Ma, Buffett, and Trump. Each step links him with a bigger name.
4. **Claiming labels first:** Repeated use of age-based firsts and superlatives, including Hupan’s first 1990s-born graduate, youngest Chinese commercial astronaut, and youngest on Forbes Asia’s 30 Under 30 list.

## 12. Core Contradictions

### 12.1 Decentralization vs. Centralized Control

- **Claim:** Building a decentralized internet infrastructure.
- **What the sources report:** Sun personally controls most TRON tokens. Nominally he is an HTX adviser, but legal documents describe him as exercising “effective control” and “common ownership” over HTX, Poloniex, and other entities.
- **Outside assessment:** Critics say the ostensibly decentralized network is run by one person and his affiliated entities, making Sun both its decentralization advocate and central planner.
- **Sources:** [Medium/Global Times Singapore](https://medium.com/@GlobalTimesSingapore/justin-sun-wants-to-control-the-future-of-crypto-the-question-is-how-much-he-already-does-5ca818c38536)
- **Reliability:** Secondary (investigative reporting and legal filings).

### 12.2 Technical Innovation vs. White Paper Plagiarism

- **Claim:** TRON is a world-leading blockchain operating system.
- **What the sources report:** Independent tools found significant overlap with IPFS and Filecoin in the white paper, covering nine or more pages with similarity above 15%.
- **Response strategy:** Blamed volunteer translators without addressing the allegedly copied material directly.
- **Sources:** [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/ryanmac/cryptocurrency-white-paper-plagiarism-tron-justin-sun)
- **Reliability in the notes:** Independent comparison of the original documents.

### 12.3 Advocating Transparency vs. Opaque Reserves

- **Claim:** Publicly criticized Coinbase’s cbBTC for a lack of transparency.
- **What the sources report:** Sun’s Poloniex could not provide proof of reserves, leaving questions about $1.3 billion in BTC. USDD removed $732 million in bitcoin collateral without fanfare.
- **Sources:** [Protos](https://protos.com/poloniex-and-the-1-3b-bitcoin-question/) / [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/defi/justin-sun-responds-after-usdd-stablecoin-removes-bitcoin/)
- **Reliability:** Primary (on-chain data) and secondary (investigative reporting).

### 12.4 Rejecting Traditional Finance vs. Embracing Political Power

- **Claim:** Decentralization, trustlessness, and code as law.
- **Sequence reported:** Invested $75 million in the Trump family’s crypto project → the SEC paused the case after Trump took office → $10 million settlement. The chronology alone does not establish causation.
- **Later reversal:** In September 2025, World Liberty Financial froze more than $100 million of Sun’s WLFI tokens. Sun publicly appealed for their release.
- **Sources:** [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/02/28/business/crypto-mogul-trump-coins-civil-fraud-charges) / [Fortune](https://fortune.com/crypto/2025/09/05/justin-sun-donald-trump-world-liberty-financial-blacklisted-crypto-address-wlfi/) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/09/05/trump-justin-sun-world-liberty-financial)
- **Reliability in the notes:** SEC filings, on-chain data, and public reporting.

### 12.5 SEC Allegations (March 2023 to March 2026 Settlement)

- **Plaintiff:** United States Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).
- **Defendants:** Justin Sun, Tron Foundation Limited, BitTorrent Foundation Ltd., and Rainberry Inc.
- **Main allegations:**
  1. Unregistered securities offerings (TRX and BTT).
  2. $31 million in allegedly unlawful proceeds from secondary-market TRX sales.
  3. Directing staff to carry out more than 600,000 wash trades in TRX between April 2018 and February 2019, trading 4.5 million to 7.4 million TRX daily.
  4. Paying celebrities to promote tokens while requiring them not to disclose their compensation. The case involved eight celebrities, including Lindsay Lohan, Jake Paul, Soulja Boy, Lil Yachty, Ne-Yo, and Akon.
- **Outcome:** The SEC ended the litigation under the Trump administration in March 2026, and a Sun-affiliated company agreed to a $10 million settlement.
- **Sources:** [SEC.gov](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023-59) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/sec-ends-case-against-crypto-billionaire-justin-sun) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/03/22/sec-hits-jake-paul-lindsey-lohan-justin-sun-with-crypto-violations.html)
- **Reliability:** Primary (official SEC documents).

## 17. Conclusion

Sun’s public writings and systematic work present an internally consistent narrative with five successive layers:

1. **Outsider who beat the odds** (2017 autobiography) → 2. **Technology evangelist** (white paper and monograph) → 3. **Financial freedom mentor** (Ximalaya and speeches) → 4. **Global elite collector** (art collection worth hundreds of millions) → 5. **Political player** (diplomat and presidential dinner guest).

Yet the story has structural contradictions: decentralization versus centralized control, technical innovation versus alleged white paper plagiarism, transparency versus opaque reserves, and rejecting traditional finance versus embracing political power.

In the notes’ assessment, Sun’s core skill is not technological innovation but **shaping the attention economy**. As Sun Studies puts it, controversy has value in itself, and consensus and attention determine everything. His approach rests on his ability to keep acting and maintain a coherent narrative under extreme pressure, including an SEC lawsuit and global mockery. One analyst described that trait as “virtually impossible to transplant.”
