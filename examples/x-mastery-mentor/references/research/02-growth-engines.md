### 1.2 The Content Flywheel

Sahil's flywheel has three layers:

**Layer 1: Content consumption feeds creation**
- Spend an hour each morning taking in newsletters, blogs, podcasts, and tweets.
- Take notes whenever something is interesting.
- Use the accumulated ideas to write in the afternoon.
- Philosophy: Write about things you genuinely want to learn. Even if a piece does not take off, it does not feel like wasted time.
- Sources: [Jakob Greenfeld](https://jakobgreenfeld.com/sahil)

**Layer 2: Platform funnel**
- Twitter is the discovery engine at the top of the funnel and the largest low-cost acquisition channel.
- Newsletter and podcast deepen relationships and retain a directly reachable audience.
- LinkedIn and Instagram add distribution channels, each exceeding 250,000 followers within months.
- Sources: [Nathan Barry Podcast #064](https://nathanbarry.com/064-sahil-bloom-unlock-growth-creator/)

**Layer 3: Turning attention into business revenue**
- The attention earned on Twitter, the newsletter, and the podcast feeds revenue-generating projects.
- Those projects include a job board, courses, advisory services, and service companies under SRB Holdings.
- All newsletter ad revenue goes back into growth through paid ads and SparkLoop at $1.50 per subscriber.
- Sources: [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/how-sahil-bloom-built-a-newsletter-that-makes-70000-a/467228)

### 1.3 Content Creation System

**Notion board** (five columns):
1. Raw Ideas
2. Upcoming
3. Work in Progress (WIP)
4. Finished, Unpublished
5. Published
Sources: [Jakob Greenfeld](https://jakobgreenfeld.com/sahil)

**Thread writing process**:
- A thread takes four to eight hours, depending on how much research it requires.
- Apply the Feynman technique: Write what you know first, read research to fill the gaps, then explain it in the simplest terms.
- Share drafts with friends before publishing to catch gaps in the logic.
- Sources: [12 Growth Lessons](https://tweethunter.io/thread-finder/tibo_maker/12-twitter-growth-lessons-from-sahil-bloom)

**Content types**:
- Concept threads about paradoxes, frameworks, and razors or rules of thumb.
- Breaking news with expert interpretation.
- Evergreen pieces that people still bookmark and share months or years after publication.
- Sources: [TweetHunter](https://tweethunter.io/thread-finder/tibo_maker/12-twitter-growth-lessons-from-sahil-bloom)

### 1.5 Engagement and Community Building

**Early cold start**:
- Share threads in the replies to around 50 large accounts.
- Most drew no response, but one repost from Chamath, who had 300,000+ followers, set off the growth.
- Paul Graham's philosophy: Do things that don't scale.
- Sources: [Creator Science](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/sahil-bloom/)

**Community first**:
- Keep direct messages open and actively reply to everyone.
- Proactively connect with creators such as Nathan Barry, Shaan Puri, Nick Huber, and Julian Shapiro.
- The idea is not to win 1,000 followers all at once, but to win one follower 1,000 times.
- Sources: [Jakob Greenfeld](https://jakobgreenfeld.com/sahil)

**Building rapport**:
- Share personal photos and everyday moments to show followers the person behind the account and build different kinds of connection.
- Sources: [Creator Science](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/sahil-bloom/)

### 1.6 Monetization Paths

**SRB Holdings** (a holding company with ten cash-generating businesses):
- Paperboy: Newsletter growth agency.
- HeyFriends!: YouTube growth agency.
- Viralcuts: Short-form video editing company.
- Additional design, back-end operations, and other services.
- Model: Partner with operators to launch businesses, serve as a high-profile client, and provide a distribution advantage.
- Launched five productized services in five months and reached mid-seven-figure annual recurring revenue, with no investment or financing and profitability from day one.
- Sources: [The B2B Creator](https://theb2bcreator.com/sahil-bloom/)

**Newsletter advertising**:
- Curiosity Chronicle generates over $70,000 a month.
- Advertising revenue funds growth instead of serving as the main income source.
- Sources: [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/how-sahil-bloom-built-a-newsletter-that-makes-70000-a/467228)

**Courses**:
- A Maven course on audience building, co-taught with Blake Burge.
- Sources: [Maven](https://maven.com/blake-burge-and-sahil-bloom/audience-building-4)

**Core strategy**: Turn a cost center into a profit center, akin to the logic behind Amazon AWS.
Sources: [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/how-sahil-bloom-built-a-newsletter-that-makes-70000-a/467228)

### 1.7 Sahil's Twelve Twitter Growth Rules

1. Find your Zone of Genius and publish only in the fields you excel in.
2. Create evergreen content that stays valuable months after publishing.
3. Seize breaking news and interpret it quickly with your expertise.
4. Start with volume. It took hundreds of threads to reach a high level (the 30-for-30 plan).
5. Be a storyteller and use narrative rather than piles of data.
6. Be an educator and break down complex concepts.
7. Curate good content to provide consistent value for followers.
8. Test with one tweet first, then expand it into a thread.
9. Follow the Feynman technique and explain difficult things in the simplest language.
10. Get feedback before publishing. Small adjustments can increase results tenfold.
11. Build a content library that new readers can explore in depth.
12. Own your audience by building an email list rather than relying on an algorithm.
Sources: [TweetHunter - 12 Lessons](https://tweethunter.io/thread-finder/tibo_maker/12-twitter-growth-lessons-from-sahil-bloom)

### 2.2 The Content Operating System

**Four-step framework**:
1. **Curate**: Collect inspiration, material, and high-performing content.
2. **Templatize**: Turn successful content structures into reusable templates.
3. **Rapid Create**: Combine templates and material to produce content quickly.
4. **Distribute**: Publish across platforms with automation tools.
- Result: 10–20 high-quality posts per hour.
- Sources: [Content OS](https://learn.justinwelsh.me/content), [The Tilt](https://www.thetilt.com/business-operations/justin-welsh-tech-stack)

**Daily cadence**:
- Get up at 5:30 a.m.
- Spend two to three focused hours on content creation each morning.
- Hold two 30-minute ideation sessions per week, generating five to seven newsletter topics.
- Often batch production to write several weeks of material at once.
- Sources: [Sell Me Well](https://sellmewell.com/sale-legends/justin-welsh-million-dollar-empire/)

### 2.3 Seven Steps for Twitter Growth (44,000+ Followers in 18 Weeks)

**Step 1: Build a posting habit**
- Publish a morning tweet each day using a fixed format: Happy {Day} to X.
- Aim for consistency and daily visibility.

**Step 2: Educate the audience**
- Add afternoon posts with practical tips.
- Cover audience growth, service businesses, information products, and social media effectiveness.

**Step 3: Introduce threads**
- Start writing long-form threads after about a month.
- Threads are easier to share and spread, and showcase deeper thinking.

**Step 4: Build an engaged ecosystem**
- Network with accounts with similar follower counts.
- Do not act like a big account. Grow alongside people at the same level.

**Step 5: Comment on large accounts**
- Turn on notifications for relevant large accounts.
- Leave valuable comments on their tweets.
- Example: A one-word comment drew 6,700 impressions.

**Step 6: Analyze and adjust**
- Monitor the numbers and cut underperforming morning tweets.
- Double down on afternoon tips that work.

**Step 7: Introduce tools**
- Introduce tools for optimization only after the process works.
- Use Content OS to create six to twelve high-quality pieces at a time.

Sources: [Justin Welsh Blog](https://justinwelsh.me/blog/03052022)

### 2.4 Cross-Platform Strategy (LinkedIn → Twitter)

**Core principle**: Same content, different packaging.
- Use “business dreams” on LinkedIn and “solopreneur” on Twitter.
- LinkedIn's audience is more corporate, with bosses watching; emphasize professional development.
- Twitter's audience is more independent; emphasize independent entrepreneurship.
- Post the same material on both to extend reach and send readers to the newsletter.
- Sources: [Growth In Reverse](https://growthinreverse.com/justin-welsh/)

**PAIPS writing formula** (a PAS variant for LinkedIn and Twitter):
- **P**ain: Introduce the problem.
- **A**gitate: Emphasize the consequences.
- **I**ntrigue: Create curiosity (“What if I told you…”).
- **P**ositive future: Show a better future.
- **S**olution: Provide the answer.
- Example: One PAIPS post drew 276,000 impressions.
- Sources: [Justin Welsh on X](https://x.com/thejustinwelsh/status/1666056673436336128)

## 3. Comparing Growth Strategies by Stage

### Zero to 1,000 Followers

| Strategy | Sahil's approach | Justin's approach | General best practice |
|------|-------------|--------------|--------------|
| Main task | Write many threads for practice and find your voice | Build a daily posting habit (Happy X to Y) | Post 3–5 times a day and engage with 20+ accounts |
| Cold start | Share your own threads in comments on 50 large accounts | Comment on similarly sized accounts and larger ones | Pin an introductory thread or high-performing tweet |
| Time investment | An hour of reading or listening daily, plus 4–8 hours writing a thread | 2–3 hours of creation each day | Spend 70% of the time engaging and 30% creating |
| Key metric | Repost rate | Daily visibility | 10–15% conversion from profile visit to follow |

Sources: [Postel](https://www.postel.app/blog/How-to-Grow-Your-X-Account-To-500-Followers-in-2025-A-Step-by-Step-Guide), [Social Rails](https://socialrails.com/blog/how-to-grow-on-twitter-x-complete-guide)

### 1,000 to 10,000 Followers

| Strategy | Sahil | Justin | General |
|------|-------|--------|------|
| Improve content | Expand from threads alone to breaking-news analysis | Add long threads to demonstrate depth | Threads get 3× the engagement of single tweets |
| Build community | Connect with creators and grow together | Build an engaged ecosystem | Support accounts at your own level |
| Distribution | Twitter and newsletter | Cross-post on Twitter and LinkedIn | Start building an email list |

### 10,000 to 100,000+ Followers

| Strategy | Sahil | Justin | General |
|------|-------|--------|------|
| Content system | Five-column Notion board | Four-step Content OS | Systems beat inspiration alone |
| Start monetizing | Newsletter ads and SRB Holdings services | Courses and newsletter sponsorships | Turn knowledge assets into products |
| Automation | Reinvest content proceeds in growth through SparkLoop and other tools | Full Hypefury automation | Bring in tools after establishing the workflow |
| Growth lever | Feynman-style evergreen content keeps getting discovered | Use PAIPS to produce high-performing content in batches | Threads are the top growth driver |

## 5. Growth Engine Patterns to Reuse

### Model A: Sahil's Flywheel and Holding Company
```
Consume content → Write threads → Twitter reach → Newsletter subscriptions → Ad revenue
                                                          ↓
                                            Reinvest in growth (SparkLoop and paid ads)
                                                          ↓
                                            Larger following → Customers for service companies
```
Best suited to people with industry expertise, a commitment to quality content, and the ability to build businesses with partners.

### Model B: Justin's Operating System and Digital Products
```
LinkedIn and Twitter → Repackage content for each → Newsletter → Course sales
      ↑                                                    ↓
 Batch with Content OS ← Automate with Hypefury ← Optimize with analysis ← Reinvest income
```
Best suited to people who prioritize efficiency, prefer productization, and want a high-margin solo business.

### General Growth Formula
1. **The first 18 minutes are critical**: Most engagement is determined within 18 minutes of posting.
2. **A repost is worth 20 likes**: The algorithm strongly favors reposts.
3. **External-link penalty**: Posts with external links reach 50–90% fewer people.
4. **Ideal hashtag count**: One or two. More than three reduces engagement.
5. **New variables in 2026**: Video and Premium offer the strongest growth leverage.

Sources: [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/twitter-algorithm/), [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/blog/social-media/x-twitter-marketing-benchmarks/), [Tweet Archivist](https://www.tweetarchivist.com/twitter-marketing-strategy-guide-2025)
