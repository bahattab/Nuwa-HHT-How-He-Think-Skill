## 1. Dan Koe: The One-Person Business and Personal Branding

### 1.1 Core Idea: You Are the Business

Dan Koe's central proposition is to turn yourself into a business. Instead of finding a market and then serving it, turn your own goals, problems, and solutions into products.

**Experience Model**: You are your own customer avatar. Solve your problems, attract people like you, and systematize the solution. Imagine climbing stairs and then sending the elevator down for the people behind you.

> Primary source: [The One-Person Business Model - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/the-one-person-business-model-how-to-monetize-yourself/)

### 1.2 The Four Pillars

| Pillar | Definition | Key action |
|------|------|----------|
| **Brand** | Your story: what you are pursuing and why | Define your vision and counter-vision (the way of life you reject) |
| **Content** | Your map: guide people from point A to point B | Post consistently for 1–3 months to build brand awareness |
| **Product** | Your solution: start with a minimum viable offer (MVO) | Sell before refining. Begin with $500–$1,000 in freelance or consulting work |
| **Marketing** | Your promotion: keep your product visible | Without promotion there is no revenue, and beginner-oriented content naturally supports growth |

> Primary source: [The One-Person Business Model - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/the-one-person-business-model-how-to-monetize-yourself/)

### 1.3 A Niche of One: Create Your Niche Rather Than Finding It

Dan Koe rejects conventional niche selection: “You don't need a niche, you need a point of view.”

**Three ingredients make up your worldview and niche:**

1. **Goals**: Set them yourself rather than adopting social defaults such as retiring at 60.
2. **Problems**: The pain points that keep you from reaching the goals.
3. **Potential paths**: Clear, step-by-step systems.

**The Interest Stack method:**
- Combine multiple interests, such as fitness, philosophy, business, and lifestyle, rather than positioning yourself in a single field.
- Their intersection creates a distinct point of view and helps avoid competing on sameness.
- A narrow niche may add 5,000 followers a year. A broad niche with a unique combination can add 20,000.

**Core formula: A broad brand and narrow products**
- For content, share the philosophy, teaching, and practice of diverse interests without directly pitching products.
- For products, build static landing pages around specific problems. The products are narrower than the content.

> You're not targeting a niche. You are a niche, and your job is to persuade people to join it.

> Primary source: [How To Create Your Niche Of One - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/how-to-create-your-niche-of-one-become-nicheless/)
> Primary source: [You don't need a niche, you need a point of view - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.medium.com/you-dont-need-a-niche-you-need-a-point-of-view-0a22b5e2802b)

### 1.4 Brand Structure: The Book of Your Life

Think of your brand as a book with four chapters:

| Chapter | Content | Suggested share |
|------|------|----------|
| **Introduction** | Your origin story and how you changed | — |
| **Philosophy** | How you define a good life, what you oppose, and your strongest convictions | 40% |
| **Education** | Teach skills and knowledge related to your brand | 40% |
| **Practice** | Build step-by-step systems and named frameworks such as The 2 Hour Writer | 20% |

> Secondary source (distillation of Dan Koe’s method): [A distilled knowledge base for building a personal brand - Dan Koe method](https://digital-garden.ontheagilepath.net/a-distilled-knowledge-base-for-building-a-personal-brand-base-on-dan-koes-method)

### 1.5 Content Creation: The 2 Hour Writer System

**Core process: Walk for an hour to find inspiration, then spend an hour writing and editing.**

**Six steps for creating content:**
1. Pick an idea from a book, podcast, or social media.
2. Ask at least three questions about it, such as “Why?” and “How?”
3. Write a first draft without judging it, using PAS (Problem–Amplify–Solution).
4. Review as a reader. Think like someone scrolling on their phone who needs to be entertained.
5. Anticipate objections and leave room for debate.
6. Format the draft: add line breaks, polish the hook, and cut filler.

**Idea Museum**:
- Keep an organized reference library. Favor old or overlooked books, carefully selected blogs, and high-quality accounts.
- Choose topics where likely performance intersects with your own excitement.

**Writing frameworks at a glance:**
- **Listicle**: Hook, list of points, then conclusion.
- **Short posts (one or two sentences)**: A personal redefinition, an uncomfortable truth, or an if-then observation.
- **P-S-B story arc**: Pain point, solution, then benefit.

> Primary source: [The 2 Hour Writer](https://2hourwriter.com/)
> Secondary source: [Dan Koe's 2-Hour Writer summary](https://ilyaas.substack.com/p/dan-koes-2-hour-writer)

### 1.6 The Content Repurposing Flywheel

**Core principle: Start from the newsletter. Spend 30 minutes a day writing it and finish the rest of the content in ten minutes.**

**Workflow:**

```
Newsletter (one or two long pieces a week)
    ├── Extract 5–7 short posts → Twitter/X
    ├── Screenshots of Twitter posts → Instagram/LinkedIn
    ├── Read the newsletter aloud → YouTube video
    ├── YouTube audio → Podcast platforms
    └── High-engagement tweets → Expand into the next newsletter topic
```

**Schedule:**
- Sunday: Outline the newsletter (30–60 minutes).
- Monday to Friday: Write a newsletter section each day (30–60 minutes), schedule daily posts (30 minutes), and engage and promote (30 minutes).
- Saturday: Publish the newsletter and write promotional posts.

**Key strategy: Test on Twitter first.** Character limits force you to distill the essence. Expand ideas that work on Twitter into a newsletter, then turn the newsletter into a YouTube script.

> Primary source: [Dan Koe Twitter](https://x.com/thedankoe)
> Secondary source: [Dan Koe's Newsletter Repurposing Content System](https://mikeromaine.com/dan-koes-newsletter-repurposing-content-system/)

### 1.7 Specific Twitter Growth Strategies

**Writing:**
- The hook is central. Spend as much time on it as on everything that follows.
- Followers do not want more content. They want solutions to problems.
- A sharp point of view makes you stand out from interchangeable posts.

**Growing through engagement:**
- **DJ method**: Make a thread curating other people's best posts (for example, “Most people do X badly. Here are seven top posts you can work through in five minutes”). Tag the authors so they repost and expose you to their followers.
- **Joining a tribe**: Reply to target accounts daily, take the discussion into DMs, ask how they grow, then propose mutual support through engagement.
- **High-quality replies**: Replace “Great post” with a 200–400-character miniature newsletter-style response.
- **Long content builds trust**: Short content earns visibility. Long content such as newsletters and articles builds deeper trust and authority.

**Seven steps for DM networking without begging:**
1. Find people whose goals genuinely align with yours.
2. Send a specific compliment about their work.
3. Ask about their goals or projects.
4. Give value first by sharing resources or making introductions.
5. Optionally move to a call or a deeper conversation.
6. Follow up with relevant resources.
7. Make a request only once the relationship is solid.

> Primary source: [The Best Way To Grow On Social Media - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/the-best-way-to-grow-on-social-media-from-zero-followers-experience/)
> Primary source: [How To Actually Grow On Social Media - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/how-to-actually-grow-on-social-media-what-they-dont-tell-you/)

## 2. Alex Hormozi: Value First and the Content Machine

### 2.1 Core Philosophy: Give Away the Secrets, Sell the Implementation

Hormozi's content philosophy in one sentence: **Share the secrets for free and charge for execution.**

- Only 1% will do it themselves. The other 99% are willing to pay someone to do it for them.
- Provide high-value content for free → prove you have a solution → create trust and reciprocity → get conversions naturally.
- He began using the strategy in 2022. He spent nothing on advertising and gained over a million followers in six months.

**Follower counts as of August 2025:**
- YouTube: 3.55 million.
- Instagram: 4 million.
- X/Twitter: 918,000.
- LinkedIn: 714,000.

> Primary source: [Alex Hormozi on X - "Giveaway the secrets, sell the implementation"](https://x.com/AlexHormozi/status/1529553072136998914)
> Secondary source: [Content Repurposing Strategy: 180k to 1.2M Followers - Copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/content-repurposing/)

### 2.2 Hook–Retain–Reward

Hormozi's content strategy rests on three stages:

**1. HOOK — Capture attention instantly**

| Technique | Example |
|----------|------|
| Shock or surprise | Offer a counterintuitive fact or figure |
| Provocative question | Prompt curiosity with a question |
| Bold promise | Offer a clear, achievable benefit |

> Example: “Most people waste 80% of their marketing budget without even knowing it.”

**2. RETAIN — Keep delivering value**
- Use stories and open loops to keep people interested.
- Every sentence or frame must add value. Use no filler.
- Break up the rhythm with headings, bullet points, and visuals.
- Keep referring back to audience pain points.

**3. REWARD — Deliver more than promised**
- Give action advice that can be put to use immediately.
- Deliver more than you promised. Promise one bonus and give three.
- Provide free resources without friction.
- Give the audience a reason to look forward to the next piece.

> Secondary source (systematic analysis of Hormozi’s strategy): [Alex Hormozi's Content Strategy: Hook, Retain, and Reward Explained](https://itsmostly.com/blog/alex-hormozis-content-strategy-hook-retain-and-reward-explained)

### 2.3 The Value Equation: Writing an Irresistible Hook

**Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

A larger numerator and a smaller denominator make the hook more compelling.

| Variable | Role | How to strengthen it |
|------|------|----------|
| **Dream outcome** | The best result the reader could get | Paint a vivid picture of the best case |
| **Perceived likelihood** | How strongly the reader believes they can succeed | Use “step-by-step,” “proven system,” or “fail-proof” |
| **Time delay** | How soon results appear | Use a short time frame (ten days rather than three months), without making unrealistic claims |
| **Effort and sacrifice** | How much work it takes | Use “simple steps,” “easy to follow,” or “beginner-friendly” |

**Before writing a hook, assess all four variables systematically:**
1. Can you make the desired outcome bigger and feel more achievable?
2. Can you shorten the timeline and reduce the effort required?

> Secondary source: [How to Use Alex Hormozi's Value Equation to Write Viral Hooks](https://davidschwertfeger.com/newsletter/alex-hormozis-value-equation-to-write-viral-hooks/)
> Primary source (book): Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers*

### 2.4 The MAGIC Headline Formula

From *$100M Offers*. It is designed for product and service names and also works for content headlines.

| Letter | Meaning | Description |
|------|------|------|
| **M** | Magnet | Give a reason to pay attention, such as a back-to-school discount or a limited-time offer |
| **A** | Avatar | Name the target audience explicitly and as specifically as possible |
| **G** | Goal | What the customer seeks: no pain, double the profit, or a first customer |
| **I** | Interval | How long will it take? “Pain-free in ten days” |
| **C** | Container | A word that shows this is a package: Challenge, Blueprint, or Bootcamp |

**Key rule: Two or three elements are enough. You do not need all five. Keep the name as short and specific as possible.**

> Primary source (book): Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers*
> Secondary source: [Alex Hormozi's MAGIC Headline Copywriting Formula - Breakthrough Marketing Secrets](https://www.breakthroughmarketingsecrets.com/blog/alex-hormozis-magic-headline-copywriting-formula-from-the-100m-offers-book/)

### 2.5 Hooks by Audience Awareness Stage

Hormozi changes the hook based on what the audience already knows:

| Awareness stage | Hook type | Example direction |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **Unaware** | Curiosity-driven | Make people ask, “What is that?” |
| **Problem-aware** | Problem-focused | Highlight the pain point and the solution |
| **Solution-aware** | Solution comparison | Explain why your solution is better |
| **Product-aware** | Competitor comparison | Compare directly with competitors |

> Secondary source: [How I Write Hooks That Actually Work - Alex Hormozi's Proven Method](https://solopreneurcode.substack.com/p/how-i-write-hooks-that-actually-work)

### 2.6 Twitter as a Content Testing Ground

**Hormozi sees Twitter as the lowest-cost place to validate ideas:**

1. Post five tweets a day to test different ideas.
2. See which ideas get the highest engagement.
3. Expand high-engagement tweets into long videos.
4. Cut the long videos into 30+ shorts for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts.

**Content style:**
- Concise, direct business advice phrased as memorable lines.
- Use short parallel sentences, then a longer closing sentence to sum them up.
- Money-making tweets do best, contrasting how rich and poor people think.
- Tuesday is a high-volume publishing day.

> Primary source: [Alex Hormozi X Profile](https://x.com/AlexHormozi)
> Secondary source: [Who's Alex Hormozi? - Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/blog/whos-alex-hormozi)

### 2.7 The Repurposing Machine: From Seven to 80 Pieces per Week

**Growth in six months:**
- YouTube: 70,000 to 300,000.
- Twitter: 10,000 to 100,000.
- Instagram: 70,000 to 330,000.
- SEO traffic: Zero to 22,000 a month.
- Podcast downloads: 20,000 to 150,000 a month.

**Five-person repurposing team:**

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|------|
| Twitter editor | Optimize and schedule Twitter content |
| YouTube editor | Edit long YouTube videos |
| LinkedIn editor | Adapt content to LinkedIn |
| Podcast editor | Produce and distribute the podcast |
| Reels/TikTok editor | Clip and optimize short videos |

**Total cost: $40,000 a month.**

**Four-step workflow:**
1. **Validate**: Test ideas cheaply on Twitter.
2. **Create**: Start with video. Video can be broken down into audio, text, and images, but the reverse is not possible.
3. **Edit and contextualize**: Have the team cut clips for each platform and insert calls to action at the beginning, middle, or end.
4. **Distribute and monitor**: Publish on a fixed schedule with tools such as Buffer.

> Secondary source: [Content Repurposing Strategy: 180k to 1.2M Followers - Copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/content-repurposing/)

## 4. Practical Frameworks for Huasheng's X Strategy

### Framework 1: Three Steps for Brand Positioning (Dan Koe)

1. **Define your vision and counter-vision**: What life do you want? What do you oppose?
2. **List your Interest Stack**: AI programming, independent development, content creation, and personal growth intersect to yield a distinct point of view.
3. **Write The Book of Your Life**: Origin story, philosophy, educational content, and practical system.

### Framework 2: Content Creation Flywheel (Combining Both Approaches)

```
Twitter posts (three to five a day to test ideas)
    │
    ├── High engagement → Long newsletter or official WeChat article
    │                    │
    │                    ├── YouTube video script
    │                    └── Xiaohongshu image-and-text post
    │
    └── Low engagement → Rewrite from another angle or drop it
```

### Framework 3: Hook Checklist (Hormozi)

Ask yourself before writing each hook:
- [ ] Is the dream outcome clear? Can readers picture the best result?
- [ ] Is it credible? Is there data, a case, or a system behind it?
- [ ] Is the timeline specific? A shorter one is more compelling.
- [ ] Is the barrier low? Do readers believe they can do it?

### Framework 4: Building Authority

| Stage | Strategy | Source |
|------|------|------|
| 0–1,000 followers | DJ method (curate others' material) and join a tribe (thoughtful replies) | Dan Koe |
| 1,000–10,000 followers | Daily posts, weekly newsletter, and sharp points of view | Dan Koe and Hormozi |
| 10,000–100,000 followers | Repurposing machine, expansion into video, and free high-value resources | Hormozi |
| 100,000+ followers | Named frameworks, such as The 2 Hour Writer, and a product ecosystem | Both |
