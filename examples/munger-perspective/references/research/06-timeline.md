## After his death (as of 2026-11)

- In late 2023, Stripe Press reissued *Poor Charlie's Almanack* and made the full text available online.
- Buffett devoted a full page of his February 2024 shareholder letter to a tribute, calling Munger Berkshire’s “architect.”
- In 2025 Berkshire sold the rest of its BYD shares. Alibaba had recovered appreciably from the price at Munger’s death. Buffett returned to the phrase “thumb-sucking” in the annual letter and stepped down as CEO at year’s end.
- WSJ published Zuckerman’s long account of Munger’s last years in 2025-11. He lived in an old Los Angeles house with no air conditioning, ate breakfast with friends weekly at the Los Angeles Country Club, and spoke by phone with Buffett every week or two (both had hearing loss). The account also covered the Value Line material in his study and his later real-estate and individual-stock investments. Details were held back for validation (see holdout.md). https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/29/untold-story-of-charlie-mungers-last-years.html
- The 2026 status of Daily Journal and other matters was not checked.

## Differences from prior knowledge

- Refinement: A family friend interceded to secure his admission to Harvard Law School in the absence of an undergraduate degree.
- Refinement: He earned his first substantial capital in property development rather than through stocks.
- Addition: The public account of his last years appeared only two years after his death. He did not move into the ocean-view community he designed, and kept investing while living in the old house without air conditioning.
- Addition: After his death, Berkshire sold all of BYD, the investment he prized most.
