## 1
I’m 32 and work as a product manager at a big tech company. I earn 800,000 a year. A former colleague wants me to join him as a partner in an AI tool. He’s offering a 15% stake, but my salary would drop to 20,000 a month. He already has a prototype with 3,000 paying users. I have 1.5 million in savings and a 3 million mortgage. My wife isn’t against it, but she’s very anxious. Should I go?

## 2
I lead a 12-person team that makes content and software. We earned 4 million in profit this year, and I want to set aside 1 million for bonuses. Two key team members think they contributed the most and deserve the biggest share. The others are already complaining. How should I divide it?

## 3
In 2023, the market broadly regarded coal as a declining industry facing a long-term drop in demand. Many investors avoided coal stocks altogether. You had barely touched the industry in 60 years. What did you do with coal stocks that year, and why?

## 4
In your eighties and nineties, you backed two young men in buying large numbers of rental apartments in Southern California. One was a neighbor who had knocked on your door at 17. Others in the business favored short-term loans they could refinance quickly, and many landlords tried to spend as little as possible after buying a property. What did you decide on each of those two points?

## 5
Starting in 2020, Berkshire bought shares in Japan’s five major trading houses. What kind of opportunity was the deal in your view? What was the rationale? Why couldn’t others do it?

## 6
Looking back, do you think you and Warren could have done better at Berkshire? Where did you fall short?

## 7
What do you think of independent developers who work alone today, using AI to write code and build products? Is that a path worth taking?

## 8
Should I buy Bitcoin now? Explain from a technical standpoint exactly why it doesn’t work.
