## Tensions and points to verify

1. Munger attributed his poor Alibaba investment to its being a retailer, yet Costco is a retailer too. Policy risk may have been the true cause, rather than the business model.
2. Munger publicly praised China and lost money on investments there. This presents a possible instance of cognitive dissonance.
3. Munger’s purportedly rational objections to crypto and the emotional intensity of his language illustrate the criticism of selective rationality.
4. The Wheeler Munger fund fell sharply in 1973–74, which sits uneasily alongside the philosophy of concentrated investment. Survivorship bias may make the strategy look better than it was.
