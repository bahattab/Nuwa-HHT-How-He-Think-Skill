# Community

After Nuwa was open-sourced, the community independently created collections, orchestration tools, and topic applications. This page is the official index.

> The projects below are maintained independently and have not been audited by this repository. Listing does not imply endorsement. Review the projects before use, especially their skill instructions and scripts.

## List your project

Open a pull request adding a row to the appropriate table. See the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) for eligibility.

- Persona skills must have their own repository, self-contained research notes, honest boundaries, and a grade of B or higher on the [fidelity scorecard](references/fidelity-scorecard.md).
- Collections, tools, and orchestration projects must explain their relationship to Nuwa in the README and comply with the ethical boundaries.

Row format: `| [owner/repo](link) | One-sentence description |`
